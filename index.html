I'm a physics PhD student at JNCASR, Bangalore, working in computational statistical mechanics. My research interests include phase transitions, critical phenomena, aging and ordering kinetics, particularly in systems with long-range interactions.

I spend a lot of my time thinking about computation, from writing scientific code and running numerical simulations to understanding what happens underneath the abstractions I use every day. • about

I'm interested in the parts of physics where computation is not just a tool for getting an answer, but becomes part of the way you explore the problem. My work involves numerical simulation and statistical mechanics, and I'm particularly interested in phase transitions, critical phenomena, aging and ordering kinetics in systems with long-range interactions.

Outside of research, I mostly work in C++ and Julia. I enjoy understanding what sits underneath the abstractions I use. Abstractions are incredibly useful most of the time, the whole point is that you don't need to care about what happens underneath but I find it satisfying to pull the layers apart and understand how things actually work.

The same goes for performance. I like knowing where the time and memory are going, what the hardware is actually doing, and how much can be gained by changing the way a piece of code is written. Sometimes there is a good reason to optimise something; sometimes I just want to see how fast I can make it.

• hobby projects

Things I make outside of research.

A collection of things I've worked on in my free time, mostly because I found them interesting enough to spend time on.

2D XY Model

Julia

A Monte Carlo study of the two-dimensional XY model using the Metropolis–Hastings algorithm. The project looks at quantities including energy, magnetization, susceptibility and heat capacity across temperature.

source ↗

Ising Model

Julia

Monte Carlo simulations of the Ising model, including equilibrium and one-dimensional implementations. The project explores the temperature dependence of quantities such as energy and magnetization and includes visualisations of the system approaching equilibrium.

source ↗

Natural Selection

Julia

A small evolutionary simulation in Julia where a population of creatures moves through a bounded environment in search of food. Speed can mutate between generations, successful creatures reproduce, and age and population size affect survival. The simulation tracks how population, speed, age and reproduction change over generations.

source ↗

x_idle_exec

C++ · X11

A tiny X11 daemon for running a command after a period of inactivity. I originally built it after looking into how xautolock detects idle time and finding that the core functionality could be handled directly through XScreenSaverQueryInfo.

source ↗

rofi-pdf-opener

Shell · Rofi

A small utility for finding and opening PDFs through Rofi. It uses fd to search and Zathura to open them, and is bound to a key so I can summon a PDF without leaving whatever I'm doing.
